## 🔬 What I do

- 🌍 **Trial-network design & G×E** — envirotyping, environmental clustering, and scenario optimization to make multi-environment trials cheaper and more informative, including under future climate
- 🧬 **Breeding simulation & genomic prediction** — stochastic breeding-program simulation (AlphaSimR) and genomic prediction on large breeding populations (~25K rice genotypes)
- 📷 **High-throughput phenotyping** — computer-vision pipelines (YOLO, SAM2, OCR) that turn fruit images into breeding-ready trait tables
- 🖥️ **Genomics on HPC** — reproducible WGS variant-discovery pipelines on LSF/SLURM clusters
- 🥒 **Polyploid breeding** — developing tetraploid cucumbers toward triploid seedless hybrids
